### 📖 What is this?

This is not your typical cookbook with chef jargon. This is a **survival guide** for:

- 🏢 Office pros who come home at 8 PM tired
- 🎒 Hostel students with one induction and one pan
- 🏠 People living alone first time
- 🍳 Complete beginners

**Every recipe follows 5 rules:** 5-10 ingredients, one pan/pot/microwave/no-cook, 5-20 min, low cleanup, beginner language.

### 📄 Food Safety

- Chicken 75°C/165°F inside no pink juices clear
- Eggs fully set
- Cooked rice not >1 hr room temp
- Leftovers refrigerate within 2 hrs
- Reheat till steaming hot
